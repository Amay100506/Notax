SafeSpace VR

Intelligent Boundary & Obstacle Alert System

An intelligent, multi-tier safety layer for WebXR and Meta Quest 3S. Actively scans physical/virtual surroundings, computes proximity vectors, provides 3D spatial cues, and delivers AI-synthesized voice warnings.

⚠️ SAFETY PROTOCOL & DEMONSTRATION DISCLAIMER This application is an advanced WebXR safety research prototype. WebXR browser APIs do not have direct raw camera feed access on consumer headsets. This system simulates intelligent obstacle proximity and boundary enforcement using spatial tracking, raycasting, and virtual bounds. It is designed to complement, not replace, Meta Quest Guardian or real-world supervision.

📖 SafeSpace VR Protocols & Help

Alert Level System

  • SAFE: No obstacles within caution threshold (> 2.4m) and inside boundary.
  • CAUTION: Obstacle detected within 2.4m - 1.5m. Gentle alert and directional cue.
  • WARNING: Obstacle detected within 1.5m - 0.75m. Pulsing HUD, directional radar compass, double-ping audio.
  • DANGER: Immediate obstacle collision risk (≤ 0.75m) or safe zone boundary breach. Red flashing strobe, alarm siren, urgent voice command.

Meta Quest 3S Controls

  • Left Thumbstick: Smooth Locomotion (Forward, Backward, Strafe)
  • Right Thumbstick: Snap Turning (30° increments)
  • Trigger: Point & click VR buttons / UI interact
  • Grip: Grab & reposition virtual obstacles in VR
  • Controller Proximity: Hand controllers independently detect obstacle distances to prevent hand collisions while swinging.

Desktop Testing Controls

  • WASD Keys: Move around the environment
  • Mouse Click & Drag: Look around in 360°
  • Shift Key: Hold to sprint faster
  • Spacebar: Test voice synthesis output
